Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Curb Guy LLC ("The Curb Guy," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you contact us, request a quote or consultation, call or text us, or use our website at mrcurber.com. By using our website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic website usage data. This may include:

  • Contact information — your name, phone number, email address, and service (home) address.
  • Project details — information you share about the work you want done, including photos or descriptions you send us.
  • Communications — the content of your calls, text messages, emails, and form submissions with us.
  • Website usage — basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected through cookies and tools like Google Analytics or advertising pixels (see "Cookies & Analytics" below).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests;
  • Prepare quotes and schedule your free consultation or service;
  • Perform and follow up on the work you hire us for;
  • Contact you about your project by phone call, text message, or email;
  • Improve our website and services.

Phone Calls & Text Messages (SMS)

When you provide your phone number — by filling out our form, calling us, or texting us — you agree that we may contact you by phone call and text message about your inquiry, quote, consultation, or service. This may include messages sent using automated technology.

Your consent & how to opt out:

  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your project.
  • You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.
  • You can ask us to stop calling or texting at any time by telling us during a call, or by emailing us.
  • Consent to receive calls or texts is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or contact information with third parties for their own marketing.

Call Recording & Monitoring

We may record or monitor phone calls with us for quality assurance, staff training, and accurate record-keeping of your project details. If call recording is active, by continuing your call with us you consent to being recorded. If you do not wish to be recorded, please let us know at the start of the call or contact us another way, such as email or text.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With trusted service providers who help us run our business — for example, our online scheduling/quote tool (Jobber) and website analytics or advertising providers (such as Google) — and only as needed to provide those services;
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and advertising pixels, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Ask what information we have about you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information;
  • Opt out of calls and texts as described above.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please review it periodically.
