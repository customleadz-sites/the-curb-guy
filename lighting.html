Installed Once. Built to Disappear by Day.

A slim channel, matched to your home

The lights sit in a patented low-profile channel that tucks up under the soffit, in a color that matches your trim. Gutters and drip edge stay untouched.

Run it from anywhere

The Trimlight Edge app sets colors, patterns and schedules from your phone, at home or out of town. Works with Google Home and Alexa.

Decades, not seasons

Bulbs are rated for up to 50,000 hours. At normal use that's 20 to 30 years, and the core components carry a lifetime product warranty.

Won't fade or yellow

No plastic lens or cover to cloud up. The LEDs face down beneath the channel, out of direct sun, so the colors stay true year after year.

Pennies to run

Each bulb draws about 0.9 watts on safe 12-volt low voltage. The controller mounts in the garage and plugs into a regular outlet.

Installed by an authorized dealer

Licensed and insured, factory-trained on the system, and backed by a 5-year labor warranty on the install on top of the product warranty.
